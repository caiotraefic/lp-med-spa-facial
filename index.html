Advanced Facial
Treatments

Eleven specialized treatments, each designed to address specific skin concerns with proven, medical-grade protocols.

01

Radio Frequency Face

Non-surgical facelift using electromagnetic heat to stimulate collagen, elastin, and new skin cells — firms and lifts sagging skin.

60 min 6–10 sessions No downtime
  • Reduces fine lines and wrinkles
  • Firms loose, sagging skin
  • Stimulates collagen production
02

Anti-Aging Facial

AHA acids, collagen-infused serums, and hyaluronic acid restore skin firmness, smooth lines, and keep complexion youthful and fresh.

60 min 4 sessions No downtime
  • Softens fine lines
  • Minimizes sun damage & melasma
  • Reduces acne marks
03

Chemical Peel

Deep exfoliation removes dead skin cells, promotes collagen production, and unclogs pores — improving texture, tone, and acne scars.

60 min 3 sessions 7–10 day recovery
  • Corrects sun damage
  • Improves acne & scarring
  • Enhances skin tone & texture
04

Carbon Peel

Carbon solution applied to skin followed by laser — deep exfoliation, acne bacteria elimination, and pore refinement in one treatment.

60 min 3–5 sessions No downtime
  • Destroys acne-causing bacteria
  • Shrinks enlarged pores
  • Reveals brighter, smoother skin
05

Dermaplaning

Gently removes dead skin cells and vellus hair for instantly smooth, glowing skin — enhanced product absorption and flawless makeup application.

60 min Monthly No downtime
  • Instantly smooth skin
  • Suitable for all skin types
  • Reduces fine lines appearance
06

Signature Facial

Cleansing, exfoliation, extractions, mask, booster serums, and infrared light — the ultimate relaxing, customized rejuvenation experience.

60 min Monthly No downtime
  • Fully customized to your skin
  • Prevents premature aging
  • Stimulates cell renewal
07

Hydrodermabrasion

Non-invasive treatment combining deep cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, hydration, and moisturization in a single rejuvenating session.

60 min Monthly No downtime
  • Removes dead skin cells
  • Treats pimples and acne
  • Brightens and rejuvenates
08

LED Light Therapy

Anti-aging and anti-inflammatory LED treatment that boosts collagen, kills bacteria, and promotes circulation for smoother, clearer skin.

60 min 4 sessions No downtime
  • Boosts collagen production
  • Reduces inflammation & acne
  • Smoother, healthier skin
09

Fibroblast Tags Removal

Minimally invasive plasma procedure precisely targeting skin tags without cutting — minimal bleeding and reduced infection risk.

60 min 1–2 sessions 7–10 day recovery
  • Precise plasma cauterization
  • No sutures required
  • Minimal bleeding
10

Microneedling / Dermapen

Micro-channels trigger collagen and elastin production, dramatically improving scars, fine lines, stretch marks, and overall skin texture.

60 min 3–6 sessions 7–10 day recovery
  • Reduces acne scars
  • Firmer, more youthful skin
  • Corrects hyperpigmentation
11

Men Facial

Targeted skincare for men's unique skin needs — combating clogged pores, blackheads, oiliness, and signs of aging with customized protocols.

60 min Monthly No downtime
  • Controls excess oil & pores
  • Minimizes signs of aging
  • Customized for men's skin
